Pipeline Architecture
Step 1 • Preprocessing Pipeline
Morphological & Stopword Filtering
Regex cleaning, case normalization, NLTK WordNet lemmatization, and pruning of domain boilerplate words.
Step 2 • Vectorization
Document-Term Representation (DTM)
Constructs a 200 x 705 Bag-of-Words matrix with min_df=2 and max_df=0.85 (97.52% sparsity).
Step 3 • Latent Dirichlet Allocation
Online Variational Bayes
Approximates intractable Dirichlet posterior mixtures by iteratively maximizing the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO).
Step 4 • Evaluation & Coherence
U_mass Topic Coherence & Perplexity
Validation using pairwise co-occurrence log-ratios across top keywords (-1.875 coherence).
Mathematical Formulation
Joint Probability Distribution:
P(W, Z, θ, β | α, η) = ∏ P(β_k | η) ∏ P(θ_d | α) ∏ P(z_dn | θ_d) P(w_dn | β_z_dn)

Document-Topic Dirichlet Prior (α = 0.1): Low α enforces that each document is governed by only a small subset of topics.

Topic-Word Dirichlet Prior (β = 0.01): Low β ensures that each latent topic concentrates on distinct vocabulary terms.

U_mass Coherence Metric:
C(z) = ∑ ∑ log [ ( D(w_m, w_l) + 1 ) / D(w_l) ]

Media & Editorial Impact

In high-volume digital newsrooms, this unsupervised topic modeling pipeline provides three critical capabilities:

  1. Automated Content Tagging: Articles are indexed across multiple tags without requiring human journalists to manually tag stories.
  2. Agenda & Bias Monitoring: News editors can measure temporal topic frequency to detect underreported stories or disproportionate focus.
  3. Contextual Article Recommendations: Recommending stories via cosine distance in topic space achieves higher semantic relevance than simple keyword matches.
